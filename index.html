About

I am an assistant professor in the School of Accountancy at the BYU Marriott School of Business. My research examines the real consequences of tax policy, corporate investment and tax planning, and how firms use disclosure to learn from stock prices. My work has appeared in The Accounting Review, Review of Accounting Studies, and Contemporary Accounting Research.

I earned my bachelor's degree at Utah Tech University, my master's degree at BYU, and my doctorate at the University of Oregon, all in accounting. At Oregon, I received the Doctoral Fellowship for Outstanding Doctoral Student. Before joining BYU in 2022, I was on the faculty at Texas A&M University's Mays School of Business.

My dissertation received the American Accounting Association's 2025 Competitive Manuscript Award.
